## What the replay says, and what it does not

"No regression" above means: on the legacy client corpus over `/api/v1/graphql`,
the new image answers exactly as the previous Chronos image did. The corpus
does not exercise the native-aware documents the Chronos dev client sends, nor
the REST or MCP surfaces beyond the smokes above; those remain covered by the
unit and integration suites CI ran, not by a live replay.

The 48 failures are the ones §5.1 classifies: roots not on the kernel by
decision (B/F1, client migration is the gate), the native INS dimension-slug
contract, the undeclared INS value-level deltas awaiting an allowlist decision,
and data-vintage drift. Two allowlist entries matched no difference any more;
WP1 below re-pinned them.
