# Slice 1 deletion map — retiring the legacy entrypoint surface

Date: 2026-09-09 · Branch: `fix/chronos-review-decisions-2026-09-09` · Read-only inventory.

Scope as briefed: slice 1 deletes the legacy budget-viz GraphQL modules, the legacy INS
module, the legacy MCP module and GPT REST, the legacy `/graphql` schema and endpoint, the
legacy env keys, and the legacy-only files under `src/app/`, while KEEPING `src/api.ts` +
`src/app/build-app.ts` as a slim composer for the product/platform modules plus the embedded
redesign kernel surface (`registerRedesignSurface`). Slice 2 ports the platform modules to
the kernel build and deletes `api.ts`.

Six of the briefed deletions are not reachable in slice 1 as specified. They are stated as
blockers with evidence, not silently dropped or silently widened.
